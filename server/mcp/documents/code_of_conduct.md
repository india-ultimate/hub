_(Applies to Adults and Minors) · Issued by India Ultimate_

This Code of Conduct applies to all stakeholders, including players, coaches, staff, volunteers, organisers, and partners. It establishes the standards of behaviour required to ensure a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment in all Ultimate activities, events, and interactions.

## 1. Commitment to Safeguarding

All stakeholders share responsibility for maintaining environments that prioritise safety, dignity, respect, and wellbeing. Participation in any activity is conditional upon adherence to this Code.

## 2. Standards of Conduct for All Participants

All individuals are expected to behave in ways that uphold respect, safety, and inclusion at all times.

Everyone must treat others with dignity and fairness and must not discriminate against, harass, or exclude any person. Language and behaviour must remain respectful and free from abuse, threats, sexual remarks, or discriminatory expression.

Personal boundaries must be respected. Individuals must not pressure others into activities, interactions, or conversations, and must not engage in bullying, intimidation, teasing, humiliation, rumour-spreading, or the sharing of private information without permission.

Participation in activities must prioritise safety. All participants are expected to follow safety instructions, remain aware of the wellbeing of others, and stop activity if someone is injured, distressed, or uncomfortable.

Communication, including digital communication, must remain respectful and appropriate. Images, videos, or personal information must not be shared without consent, and harmful or offensive content must not be created, distributed, or endorsed.

All participants share responsibility for safeguarding. Concerns about unsafe or inappropriate behaviour must be raised promptly, and individuals who speak up must be supported.

## 3. Additional Responsibilities of Leaders

Individuals in positions of responsibility; including coaches, captains, organisers, committee members, and others in supervisory or decision-making roles, hold an enhanced duty of care.

Leaders must prioritise the safety and wellbeing of participants at all times and maintain clear professional boundaries in both physical and digital interactions. Unnecessary one-to-one private situations, particularly involving minors, must be avoided.

Activities must be appropriate to the age and ability of participants, and minors must be properly supervised. Environments should be open and observable wherever possible. Any private settings, including homes, must be transparent and known to parents or guardians.

Authority must be exercised responsibly and fairly. Leaders must not misuse power, show favouritism, or create dependency. Communication with minors must be appropriate, necessary, and transparent.

Safeguarding concerns must be reported promptly through appropriate channels. Concerns involving minors must always be reported. Confidentiality must be respected while ensuring safety and protection.

Leaders must model respectful conduct and sportsmanship and must not use alcohol, tobacco, vaping products, or drugs during activities or in the presence of minors.

### High-Performance Training, Recovery and Athlete Wellbeing

National team athletes and other high-performance participants are expected to train rigorously and commit fully to their sporting responsibilities. However, performance expectations must always be balanced with athlete safety, health, wellbeing, and adequate recovery.

Athletes are encouraged to communicate openly when they are experiencing excessive fatigue, pain, injury, distress, or when additional recovery or rest is reasonably required. Coaches and other leaders must take such concerns seriously and must not knowingly overwork, pressure, or expose athletes to unreasonable training loads or inadequate recovery.

No athlete should face retaliation for raising genuine concerns about training, fatigue, injury, recovery, or wellbeing, or for requesting reasonable rest.

## 4. Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviours are strictly prohibited for all stakeholders:

- Any form of physical, emotional, or sexual abuse, exploitation, or harm.
- Any sexual or romantic behaviour involving a person under the age of 18.
- Bullying, harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Misuse or unauthorised sharing of private or personal information.
- Ignoring, concealing, or suppressing safeguarding concerns.

## 5. Declaration and Agreement

By agreeing below, I acknowledge that I have read, understood, and agree to comply with this Safeguarding Code of Conduct. I understand my responsibility to contribute to a safe and respectful environment.

I acknowledge that violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action, removal from activities, suspension, reporting to relevant authorities, and/or legal action where applicable.
